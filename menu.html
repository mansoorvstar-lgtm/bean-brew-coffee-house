Our Menu

While the specialty of the house is Coffee we have a variety of both cold beverages, teas, pastries, snacks and meal to choose from that you can choose to go along your beverage of choice that you decide on while spending your time at our coffee house.

Coffee

Espresso

Espresso is a shot of coffee that has an rich flavor. It is for the person who likes coffee black with nothing added.

Americano

American coffee is made by pouring milk into an espresso. Black coffee is best enjoyed black with a flavor.

Cappuccino

Cappuccino is made with espresso,and foamed milk. It is a coffee drink that has a flavor and a creamy top.

Latte

Latte is espresso and steamed. It is a coffee drink that's smooth and mild.

Mocha

Mocha is a coffee drink made with coffee, milk and hint of chocolate. Mocha is the pick‑up for the tooth, among us.

Cold Coffee

Iced Coffee

Iced coffee is the pick‑up when a cold drink is desired. Coffee lovers, enjoy this beverage!

Cold Brew

Cold brew is a style of coffee made by soaking coffee grounds in room-temperature water for hours. The result is an rich coffee that can be enjoyed either hot or iced.

Tea & Other Drinks

We know not everyone comes to a coffee shop just to get coffee. For those who enjoy tea we also offer types of teas and other drinks in case you want something different from coffee.

Whether you are meeting friends or taking a coffee break we have something for you.
