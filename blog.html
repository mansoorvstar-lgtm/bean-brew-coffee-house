Coffee Breaks at Bean & Brew

Coffee can fit into parts of the day. Some people like to have it in the morning. Others prefer a cup during an afternoon break. It is a drink that can bring a little comfort no matter what time it is.

A Place to Spend Some Time

When you visit a coffee house a coffee house visit does not always have to mean ordering a drink and leaving. At a coffee house you might meet a friend have a conversation read for a while or simply sit and enjoy the coffee.

Bean & Brew Coffee House is designed as a place, for these everyday moments.

Try Something Different

If you always order the coffee each time your next visit can be a chance to try something new. You could choose a type of coffee order a cold drink or add a pastry to your usual coffee.

Small changes can make a familiar coffee break feel different.
